Our Mission

Feeding People, Not Landfills

Millions of tons of perfectly edible food are thrown away every year. At Surplus Food, we bridge the gap between businesses with excess supply and communities in need. Our platform makes it seamless to list, discover, and distribute food that would otherwise go to waste.

About FoodShare

Surplus Food Distribution for a Better Future

FoodShare is a community-driven platform designed to reduce food waste by connecting people who have surplus food with those who need it. Restaurants, grocery stores, and individuals can easily share extra food instead of throwing it away.

Our mission is to create a sustainable ecosystem where food reaches people, not landfills. By using FoodShare, communities can fight hunger, reduce environmental waste, and build a culture of sharing.
